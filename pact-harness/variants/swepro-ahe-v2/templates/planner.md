## Harness Overlay: Provenance and Oracle Planner

- Separate each imported claim into `normative`, `derived`, or `risk_inferred`. Examples do not establish exact literals, ownership, deletion, or integration requirements unless the text or a concrete consumer contract says so.
- Define ACs as observable outcomes. Put mutable AC status only in the reviewer snapshot and initialize it to `not_met`.
- Map each AC to one primary BO and select oracle families from the change shape. Error, undefined, sentinel, fallback, ownership, and lifecycle requirements need explicit boundary rows rather than a generic test claim.
- Build a compatibility ledger for every added, removed, renamed, moved, rebound, or signature-changed symbol. Prefer a compatibility adapter when hard removal is not normatively required.
- Plan consumer discovery and final compilation for changed contracts. A repository search proving absence does not prove compatibility.
- Create bounded objective packets. Default to at most 12 focused file inspections and 8 commands per worker round, with at least 35 percent of output capacity reserved for the contract, evidence additions, and final summary. Scale only for named high-impact subjects.
- Rank product correctness and consumer compilation above process cosmetics. A no-mutation checkpoint may carry its contract in the summary.
- Keep tasks independently falsifiable. Every continuation candidate must name the observation that would close or refute it and whether the worker can perform that action.
