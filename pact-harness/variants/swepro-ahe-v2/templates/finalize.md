- Re-run only decisive stale checks: affected-consumer compilation, selected high-impact boundary oracles, and patch integrity. Do not spend the finalize round repeating low-risk settled evidence.
- A final source inspection cannot substitute for executable negative, sentinel, error-identity, lifecycle, propagation, or consumer-compatibility evidence.

- Do not remove or rename a contract merely because the implementation no longer needs it internally. Require normative removal evidence and consumer closure, or retain a compatibility adapter.
- Do not block completion on an unconstrained exact choice. Block only when normative text, a concrete consumer, or authoritative verification makes the choice observable and required.
- No cosmetic cleanup is allowed after the decisive checks. Any mutation invalidates the affected fingerprint entries and requires focused re-verification.
- For a no-mutation finalize pass, embed a complete finalize contract in the summary if no standalone contract location is provided. A process-only file omission must not create another unchanged round.
